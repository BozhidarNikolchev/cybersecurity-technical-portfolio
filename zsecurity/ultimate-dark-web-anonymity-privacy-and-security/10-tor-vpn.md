# VPN + Tor

## Models

### VPN → Tor

- ISP sees VPN traffic only  
- Tor sees VPN IP  

---

### Tor → VPN

- More complex  
- Used in advanced setups  

---

## Benefits

✅ Adds extra anonymity layer  
✅ Hides Tor usage from ISP  

---

## Risks

❌ Requires trust in VPN provider  

---

## Key Insight

Combining layers increases privacy but adds complexity.
