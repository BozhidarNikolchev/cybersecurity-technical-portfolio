## What Are Darknet Entry Points?

Darknet entry points are methods and resources used to discover hidden services on anonymity-focused networks such as Tor.

Unlike the traditional internet, hidden services are not normally indexed by common search engines.

---

## Why Entry Points Matter

Without entry points:

- Hidden services are difficult to locate
- Onion addresses are difficult to remember
- Discovery becomes inefficient

Entry points provide a starting location for exploration.

---

## Common Entry Points

### Search Engines

Specialized search engines may index onion websites.

Examples:

- Ahmia
- Torch
- Haystak

### Directories

Collections of categorized onion links.

Examples:

- Hidden Wiki style directories
- Community-maintained listings

### Recommendations

Links shared through:

- Security communities
- Forums
- Research groups
- Trusted contacts

---

## Security Considerations

Not all discovered services are trustworthy.

Potential risks include:

- Scam websites
- Phishing pages
- Malicious content
- Fake marketplaces

Verification should always be performed before interacting with a service.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Darknet resources require specialized discovery methods.
- Search engines and directories act as entry points.
- Hidden services are not easily discovered through traditional search engines.
- Validation and caution are essential.
