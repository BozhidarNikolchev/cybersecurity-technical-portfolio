# Onion Services (Hidden Services)

## What are Onion Services?

Onion services are services hosted inside the Tor network.

They use special domains:

.onion

---

## Key Characteristics

- No DNS involvement  
- No public IP exposure  
- Fully encrypted communication  

---

## How They Work

Both the user and the service communicate entirely inside the Tor network through encrypted circuits.

---

## Benefits

✅ Full anonymity for hosts and users  
✅ No direct exposure to the public internet  
✅ Enhanced privacy  

---

## Key Insight

Onion services remove the need to trust traditional internet infrastructure.
