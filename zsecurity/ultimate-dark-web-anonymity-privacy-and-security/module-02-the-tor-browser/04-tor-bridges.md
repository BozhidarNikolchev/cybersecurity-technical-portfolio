# Tor Bridges

## What are Bridges?

Bridges are private Tor nodes not listed in the public directory.

---

## Purpose

To bypass censorship and detection.

---

## When to Use

- ISP blocking Tor  
- Government restrictions  
- Restricted networks  

---

## Pluggable Transports

Examples:

- obfs4  
- meek  

---

## Key Insight

Bridges make Tor traffic harder to detect.
